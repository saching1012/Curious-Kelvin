Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Curious Kelvin is a thermodynamics and fluid property exploration tool for engineers and students. This page explains what information the app collects and how it's handled.

No account, no personal information

Curious Kelvin does not require you to create an account, log in, or provide your name, email address, or any other personal information to use it.

Calculation data

Values you enter — temperature, pressure, fluid selection, and similar inputs — are used only to perform calculations during your session. They are not stored, saved to a database, or shared with any third party.

Standard hosting logs

Like virtually all websites, our hosting provider and content delivery network may automatically log standard technical information for security and performance purposes, such as IP address, browser type, and access times. This information is not used to identify individual users, and is handled according to each provider's own policy:

  • Render (hosting): render.com/privacy
  • Cloudflare (content delivery): cloudflare.com/privacypolicy

Advertising

Curious Kelvin does not display advertisements and does not share any data with advertising networks.

Children's privacy

Curious Kelvin is a general-audience educational tool, including for students learning thermodynamics. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated occasionally. Continued use of the app after a change indicates acceptance of the updated policy.
